This is a sample article. It doubles as a reference: everything a writer can do in a piece appears somewhere below.

Paragraphs are separated by a blank line. Text can be **bold** or *italic*, and a [link](https://example.com) is written with square and round brackets.

## Subheadings break up a long read

A subheading starts with two pound signs. Use them every few paragraphs in longer pieces so a reader can find their place again.

### Smaller headings are available too

Three pound signs make a smaller heading, useful for sections within sections.

> A line starting with a greater-than sign becomes a pull quote. It is set large, with a red rule beside it.

## Lists

- A line starting with a hyphen becomes a bullet point
- Lists are good for short, parallel items
- Keep each one to a line or two

1. Numbered lists work the same way
2. Start each line with a number and a full stop

## Pictures

To use a photo, add it to the folder `src/assets/img` and write its name at the top of the article file as `image: /assets/img/your-photo.jpg`. Articles without a photo get generated artwork like the one above, in the style of the Counterpoint logo.
