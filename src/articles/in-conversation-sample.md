This is a sample interview. Open with a short introduction: who the subject is, why they matter now, and where and when you spoke. Two or three sentences is enough.

### Where did this begin for you?

Answers are written as ordinary paragraphs under each question. Let the subject speak at length; the format is built for long answers.

A second paragraph of the same answer simply follows after a blank line.

### What do people most often get wrong about your work?

Questions are written as small headings, with three pound signs in front. They appear in red typewriter text so a reader can skim from one question to the next.

> The most striking line of the conversation can be pulled out like this.

### What are you working on next?

End on the question that looks forward. Then add a closing line of credit if needed, such as *This interview has been edited for length and clarity.*
