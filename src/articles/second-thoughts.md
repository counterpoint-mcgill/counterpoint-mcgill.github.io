This is a sample article, standing in for a short opinion piece.

A good opinion piece makes one claim and defends it. State the claim in the first paragraph. Give the strongest version of the other side in the second. Spend the rest showing why you still hold your view.

## The other side

Every argument worth making has a serious objection. Put it here, in terms its supporters would recognise, and answer it directly.

> The point of a counterpoint is not to win. It is to make the first voice easier to hear.

Finish by telling the reader what would change your mind.
