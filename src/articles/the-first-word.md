This is a sample article. It is here to show how a finished piece looks on the site, and it is safe to delete once the first real one is ready.

An opening note like this one usually answers three questions: who is behind the publication, what it will cover, and what a reader can expect if they come back next week. Keep it short. The work that follows will say the rest.

## What to put here

Write the way you would speak to a reader across a table. Say what Counterpoint is for, and what it is against. Name the kinds of pieces you plan to run — reported features, essays, interviews, video — and how often.

> A pull quote looks like this. Use one to lift the single line you most want a reader to remember.

Then sign off, and publish.
